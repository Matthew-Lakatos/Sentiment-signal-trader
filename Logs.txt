YEAR 1 — FOUNDATIONS
====================================================================

Q3 2023 — Initial Conception

Objective:
Build a system capable of identifying financially relevant information
from public sources before market participants fully process it.

Initial assumptions:

- Markets are not perfectly efficient.
- Information diffusion takes time.
- Narrative and sentiment influence price.
- News can be converted into quantitative signals.

Early prototypes:

- RSS scraping
- Headline collection
- Rule-based sentiment scoring
- SQLite storage
- Simple event logs

Primary challenge:

Separating financially relevant information from general news noise.

Lessons:

- Raw sentiment alone produces weak signals.
- Context matters more than polarity.

====================================================================

PHASE 3 BUG DISCOVERY

Critical finding:

Attribution decomposition failed accounting identity.

Problem:

Materiality and credibility stored separately from attribution fields.

Result:

Stored attribution components did not sum to realized P&L.

Fix:

Collapsed:

Materiality × Credibility

Into sentiment component.

Outcome:

Attribution became exhaustive.
